O-

Universal Red Blood Cell Donor

Type O-Negative blood has neither A, B, nor Rh antigens on the red blood cells, making it safe for anyone of any blood group during trauma surgeries when there is no time to test.

AB+

Universal Red Blood Cell Recipient

Type AB-Positive individuals have both A and B antigens plus the Rh factor. Therefore, their immune systems will not attack donor red blood cells from any blood group!
